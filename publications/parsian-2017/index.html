Download PDFOfficial record

Abstract

This paper describes improvements to Parsian robots' hardware and software architecture. Hardware developments include fault detection, two-way communication, and the dribbler system. Software developments include pass and interception skills, an adaptive attack strategy for regular play, and reactive offense and defense for free kicks.
